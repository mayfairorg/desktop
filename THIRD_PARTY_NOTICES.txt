Tor
===
Mayfair Desktop can start the open-source "tor" program (the Tor daemon) on your computer to
route one private tab through the Tor network. It is not Tor Browser. Mayfair Desktop is not
made, endorsed or supported by The Tor Project, Inc. "Tor" is a trademark of The Tor Project.

The tor program is downloaded unmodified from the Tor Project's official packages when this
app is built (see tools/fetch-tor.js) and is shipped as a separate program in the app's
"tor" resource folder. Source code: https://gitlab.torproject.org/tc/tor
