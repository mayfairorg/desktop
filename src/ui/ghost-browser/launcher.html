Investigation browser

A separate browser for one case. Each case window has its own temporary session that is wiped when it closes. Nothing is kept in your normal browser.

What the routes do and do not hide

  • Tor hides your IP address from the sites you visit. Tor relays and the exit relay can still see where traffic ends up, and the exit sees any traffic that is not HTTPS.
  • Direct with encrypted DNS only stops your network provider reading your name lookups. Sites and your provider still see your IP address and the sites you connect to.
  • Signing in to any account links that session to you. Fingerprint reduction narrows what pages can read but does not make this browser look like every other one.
  • Only sites you allow for the case are reached. Everything else is blocked and listed in the case window.

Kill switch

Closes every case window, wipes their sessions, stops Tor and locks the Ghost vault. Also Ctrl+Shift+Backspace (Cmd on a Mac) from any Ghost browser window.
